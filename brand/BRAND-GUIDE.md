# CyleSoma Brand Kit

**Name:** CyleSoma (powered by Cyle Technologies)
**Tagline:** Simplifying School Management. Empowering Education.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | #053065 | Emblem, "Cyle", text, headers |
| Bright Blue | #0075E7 | "Soma", buttons, links, accents |
| Sky Blue | #148FF9 | Highlights, hover states, gradients |
| Deep Navy (text) | #001E44 | Tagline and body text on white |
| White | #FFFFFF | Backgrounds, reversed logo |

## Logo files (logo/)
- cylesoma-logo-full: emblem, wordmark, tagline (transparent)
- cylesoma-logo-horizontal: emblem beside wordmark, for headers and navbars
- cylesoma-mark: emblem only, for icons, stamps, watermarks, e-ID cards
- cylesoma-wordmark: text only
- *-reversed: white and blue version for navy or dark backgrounds

## Usage rules
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" bar of the wordmark.
- Minimum size: full logo 160px wide; mark 16px (favicon) ; horizontal logo 120px wide.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects. Use the reversed files on dark backgrounds.
- Use the mark alone where space is tight (favicons, app icons, avatars).

## Where files go
- favicon/ and app-icons/: copy into your web app's public/ folder and paste head-snippet.html into <head>.
- social/: avatars, link-preview image and banner.
